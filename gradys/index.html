    Questions

    Good to know

    What's the difference between the Static Site and the Full Site?

    The Static Site is the finished site you've seen. To change the menu or a special, you text me and I update it. The Full Site adds a login so you can make those changes yourself, plus online ordering, a party booking form, an events calendar, Google reviews, an email and text sign-up, and visitor stats.

    Can I start with the Static Site and upgrade later?

    Yes. Launch the Static Site now, then add any Full Site item later at its listed price.

    Do I own the website?

    Yes. Once the build is paid, the site is yours. Your domain is registered in your name, so you're never locked in.

    What if I want to change the menu or a special?

    Just text or email me. Menu, price, special and photo changes are covered by monthly site maintenance, usually done the same day. With the Full Site you can also make them yourself.

    How long until it's live?

    Usually 1–2 days after you say yes. Most of that is just connecting your domain.

    How does the AI host work?

    It's a chat window on the site powered by the newest generation of AI models. I set it up with your menu, hours, specials and policies so it answers guests' questions accurately at any hour, and I check it monthly to keep it current. It hands party requests off to you, and it never takes payments.

    Will people find it on Google?

    The site is set up so Google can read your name, address, hours and menu. Linking it from your Google Business Profile and Instagram bio helps it show up even faster.

    Can we add online ordering or reservations later?

    Yes. The site is built so we can add ordering, a booking form, reviews or more pages whenever you're ready.

    What happens if I stop monthly maintenance?

    The site is still yours. You'd just handle hosting and updates yourself, and I'll help hand everything over.
